# Paper values read by eye

fig3_by_eye.csv holds bar heights read off Fig. 3(a)-(d) of Yang et al. (2026) by eye from the PDF. They are approximate (about +/- 10% of the axis step) and are only for side-by-side comparison. They are not data from the authors. The EKF time bars are too small to read; 0.02 ms stands for "close to zero".

fig5_7_by_eye.csv holds the curves of Figs. 5, 6 and 7 at K = 5, 10, 15, 20, read the same way (log axes: about a quarter of a decade). Two figure values disagree with the paper's text: the text says greedy angle RCRB reaches "approximately 10^1 degrees" at K = 20 (Fig. 6 shows about 0.7 deg) and HH distance RCRB is "below 0.5 m" at K = 20 (Fig. 7 shows about 2.4 m). We keep the figure values.

fig9_by_eye.csv holds Fig. 9 at 1-6 GHz. The points lie on p = 1e-3 mW x (f / 1 GHz)^2 x (D / 1 Kb) to reading accuracy.

fig5_nr1_by_eye.csv holds the two Nr = 1 curves of Fig. 5 ("Hybrid Heuristic with Nr=1" and "Benchmark with Nr=1") at K = 5, 10, 15, 20.
